# Maven Fuzzy Factory – E-commerce Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

The project analyses the sales and website activity of Maven Fuzzy Factory, an e-commerce business. It's objective is to examine business performance, understand product and customer purchasing patterns, and evaluate how effectively website sessions convert into orders.

This analysis was conducted using Python and SQL to extract meaningful insights and support business decision-making.

## 2. Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering three key business questions:

How much revenue and gross profit did the business generate?

How did products and customer purchasing behaviour contribute to sales?

How effectively did website sessions convert into orders?


## 3. Tools and Technologies

Python: Data analysis and manipulation.
Pandas: Data loading, inspection, grouping and aggregation.
Matplotlib:Data visualisation.
SQL (DuckDB): Business queries and data aggregation.
Jupyter Notebook: Python analysis environment.

## 4. Dataset Description

The project uses the Maven Fuzzy Factory e-commerce dataset, consisting of:

orders.csv – Order transactions and revenue information.

order_items.csv – Individual products included in orders.

order_item_refunds.csv – Refund records.

products.csv – Product information.

website_sessions.csv – Website session records.

website_pageviews.csv – Website pageview activity.

maven_fuzzy_factory_data_dictionary.csv – Dataset documentation.

## 6. Key Findings and Business Insights

## 6. Key Findings

### Revenue and Profitability
The business generated **$1.94 million in revenue** and **$1.22 million in gross profit**, with a gross profit margin of **62.74%**.

### Monthly Revenue Trend
Monthly revenue showed an overall upward trend, indicating growth in sales over the observed period.

### Product Performance
Product 1 was the leading primary product, contributing approximately **73.25% of analysed revenue**. Product 4 recorded the lowest revenue.

### Customer Analysis
The business recorded **31,696 unique customers**, an average order value of **$59.99**, and **591 repeat customers**.

### Website Conversion
Out of **472,871 website sessions**, 32,313 resulted in orders, representing a conversion rate of **6.83%**.

## 7. Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

Product diversification: The business should explore opportunities to strengthen sales across other products and reduce its reliance on Product 1.

Customer retention: The business should consider strategies such as loyalty programmes and targeted promotions to encourage repeat purchases.

Conversion improvement: The business should investigate the website purchasing journey to identify opportunities to improve the percentage of sessions that result in orders.

Performance monitoring: Management should continue monitoring monthly revenue, gross profit and customer purchasing behaviour to support business decisions.
